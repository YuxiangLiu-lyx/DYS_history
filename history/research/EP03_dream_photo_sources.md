身份参考：`assets/source_photos/EP03/IG2018_RIOT_MEET_TEAM.png`，来自 [Riot Worlds 2018 Meet the Teams](https://na.leagueoflegends.com/en/featured/worlds-2018-meet-the-teams)。该队面板及姓名顺序是 TheShy、Ning、Rookie、JackeyLove、Baolan，图像 873×416；只作脸和当年年龄参考，服装以冠军现场图为准。
